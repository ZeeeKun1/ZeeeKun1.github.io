About Me

Hi! I am currently a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at Nankai University, advised by Assoc. Prof. Nan Gao. My research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), particularly AI in Education and AI in Psychology.

Before beginning my Ph.D., I completed my Master's degree in Software Engineering at Nankai University under the supervision of Prof. Haining Zhang. I am also a member of the User Experience and Human-Computer Interaction Lab (SEAMi Lab).

I am always open to insightful discussions and research collaborations. Feel free to drop me an email!

Research Projects

Education

AI Kaikai & AI Zhifu: Intelligent Campus Assistants
Role: Project Lead & Core Developer
Deployed at Nankai University

Led the development of AI-driven conversational agents using single- and multi-agent architectures to serve students and counselors. Beyond providing on-demand answers to campus-related queries, the system is uniquely designed to offer preliminary psychological support and emotional scaffolding.

AI Bole · Exploration Planet (AI伯乐·探索星球)
Role: Project Lead

An AI-powered interactive platform for children's growth and potential exploration. Through game-like, scenario-based activities such as natural conversation, storytelling, challenges, and career role-play, it is designed to help children express their interests, ways of thinking, and behavioral patterns.

Project presentation
Proactive Homework Support Assistant

Developed a proactive intervention assistant that offers suggestions to parents as they help their children with homework, aiming to reduce conflicts during the process.

Psychology

Machine Learning-based Psychological Early Warning Platform
Role: Project Lead & Core Developer
Collaborated with the Mental Health Center, SEAMi Lab, Nankai University

Developed a data-driven platform to predict mental health risks among university students. By training machine learning models on massive, multi-dimensional anonymized campus records, the system assists counselors in providing timely interventions while ensuring strict data privacy.

Unobtrusive Mental Health Assessment via Daily Dialogue
Role: Project Lead & Core Researcher
Collaborated with the Mental Health Center, SEAMi Lab, Nankai University

This project explores innovative ways to passively assess students' psychological states through their natural language conversations. We aim to leverage LLMs to identify early signs of psychological distress without burdening users. We are currently developing the system prototype and preparing for the upcoming user study.

AI Companion Toy for Children

This hardware-and-software system combines voice interaction, emotion sensing, and long-term memory to provide children with ongoing companionship and conversation. A parent-facing mini program presents children's interaction data, emotional trends, and growth records, helping parents stay informed and step in when appropriate. The child, AI toy, and parent app work together as a connected support system.
